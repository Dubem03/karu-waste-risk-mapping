CRS and preparation

- All source layers arrived in EPSG:4326 (WGS 84)
- Study area: Karu LGA, Nasarawa State, Nigeria
- Study area extracted from the relevant administrative boundary dataset
- All layers clipped to the study area, then reprojected to EPSG:32632 (UTM Zone 32N)
- Area calculations were checked after reprojection using square metres and square kilometres
- Working files saved in "data/processed/"
- Raw files in "data/raw/" were left untouched
