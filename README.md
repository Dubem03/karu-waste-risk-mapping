# My GeoDev Lab Africa project

Which settlements within Karu, Aso-Kodupe and Bagaji-Agada wards of Karu Local Government Area, Nasarawa State, are potentially exposed to environmental risks associated with waste disposal sites?

This project uses geospatial and environmental datasets, including waste disposal site locations, settlement data, population data, rivers and waterways, elevation, slope, land use/land cover, rainfall, geological data, and soil information to assess potential environmental risks associated with waste disposal sites and their proximity to settlements within the three selectead wards.

The project applies GIS-based spatial analysis, including proximity and buffer analysis, to examine the spatial relationship between waste disposal sites and settlements and identify areas of potential environmental exposure. Built over twelve months with GeoDev Lab Africa, Cohort One.

See `project-brief.md` for the full project brief.
